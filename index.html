Etch pre-sale promise tracker

The Etch pre-sale and Etch's pages today

In September 2024, Digital Gravy pre-sold Etch for WordPress in three tiers, at one-off prices of $499, $799 and $1,299 (monthly instalment options were also offered), under a "strict no-refunds policy". This page sets statements from that pre-sale beside what Etch's own pages say now.

Pre-sale statements

  1. Core WordPress blocks

    Docs: "custom Gutenberg blocks (not core blocks)"

    Pre-sale

    • "Etch mirrors your exact layout & content to Gutenberg in real-time, as you build, using Core blocks."
    • "Etch doesn't trap your content in a proprietary third party environment, it liberates it into the Core of Wordpress."

    Etch pre-sale presentation slide, September 2024. Slide shown here.

    Etch's pages now

    • "Etch writes everything you build to core WordPress blocks (Auto Block Authoring) for full data liberation" (etchwp.com pricing FAQ)
    • "Etch automatically authors everything you build to core/native Gutenberg blocks in the WordPress block editor" (etchwp.com features page)
    • "everything you build is authored to custom Gutenberg blocks and stored in core WordPress" (etchwp.com Why Etch? page)
    • "Etch now authors everything you build to custom Gutenberg blocks (not core blocks)." (Etch docs, Auto Block Authoring)
    • "We tried to make core blocks behave. They wouldn't." (Etch blog, 12 November 2025)

    Checked: 6 November 2026

  2. WooCommerce integration

    Listed as not currently available

    Pre-sale

    • "Woocommerce Integration", listed as included in the Investor ($799) and Partner ($1,299) tiers and as not included in the Early-Bird ($499) tier.

    Etch pre-sale pricing page, archived 5 September 2024.

    Etch's pages now

    • "WooCommerce integration*" marked as not included on the Freelancer ($99/yr) plan, and "WooCommerce Integration*" marked as included on the Agency ($199/yr) and Agency Elite ($299/yr) plans, with the footnote: "* These features are not currently available, but are planned for the near future. Do not expect these features to be available right away." (etchwp.com pricing page)
    • "The only features planned as add-ons or restricted from lower tiers are: Woocommerce elements and templates" (etchwp.com pricing FAQ)
    • "We still have plans to build our native Woocommerce integration, but there's nothing stopping you from building an e-commerce site in EtchWP today." (etchbuilder.io pricing FAQ)

    Checked: 6 November 2026

  3. A marketplace for add-ons

    Not found on etchwp.com

    Pre-sale

    • "Etch will have a marketplace where third party developers can create add-ons for Etch. These add-ons will be fully vetted with high maintenance requirements."
    • "40% Marketplace Discount" on the Investor ($799) tier and "50% Marketplace Discount" on the Partner ($1,299) tier.

    Etch pre-sale pricing page, archived 5 September 2024.

    Etch's pages now

    • The word "marketplace" does not appear on etchwp.com's home, features or pricing pages (checked 6 November 2026).

    Checked: 6 November 2026

  4. Replacing other plugins

    Pricing page: "Replaces 6+ premium plugins"

    Pre-sale

    • "Etch will eventually replace the following: Page builders (Elementor, Divi, Bricks, Breakdance, etc.) Block themes & block systems (Kadence, Astra, Generate, etc.) CPT & custom field plugins (ACF, Metabox, etc.) Media management plugins (HappyFiles, etc.) SEO Plugins (Yoast, SEO Framework, etc.) Performance Plugins (Perfmatters, FlyingPress, etc.)"

    Etch pre-sale pricing FAQ, archived 5 September 2024.

    Etch's pages now

    • "Replaces 6+ premium plugins", listed on the Freelancer, Agency and Agency Elite plans, without an asterisk. (etchwp.com pricing page)
    • "Etch will eventually replace the following:", followed by the same six categories. (etchwp.com pricing FAQ)

    Checked: 6 November 2026

  5. SEO plugins

    Version 1.7.0: "Yoast SEO and Rank Math compatibility"

    Pre-sale

    • "Etch will eventually replace the following: [...] SEO Plugins (Yoast, SEO Framework, etc.)"

    Etch pre-sale pricing FAQ, archived 5 September 2024.

    Etch's pages now

    • "you can create Custom Post Types (CPTs) and custom fields, manage media, manage content, manage SEO optimization, and a lot more directly from within Etch." (etchwp.com pricing FAQ)
    • "Etch 1.7.0: Yoast SEO and Rank Math compatibility is here!" (Etch changelog, version 1.7.0, 8 October 2026)

    Checked: 6 November 2026

  6. Custom field plugins

    Docs: "more of a playground right now"

    Pre-sale

    • "Etch will eventually replace the following: [...] CPT & custom field plugins (ACF, Metabox, etc.)"

    Etch pre-sale pricing FAQ, archived 5 September 2024.

    Etch's pages now

    • "CPTs and custom fields", listed among core features. (etchwp.com pricing FAQ)
    • "This area of Etch is a functional Proof of Concept so that users can start to experience the tremendous advantages of a "unified workflow." It's a high priority area and will get progressively better very quickly, but the CPT/Custom Fields functionality should be treated as more of a playground right now than a real part of your workflow (unless your needs are very basic)." (Etch docs, Known Issues)

    Checked: 6 November 2026

  7. Version control

    Listed as not currently available

    Pre-sale

    • "write everything possible to actual files that can be properly versioned in Git"

    Etch pre-sale presentation slide, September 2024. Slide shown here.

    Etch's pages now

    • "Version control*" marked as included on the Agency Elite ($299/yr) plan, and marked as not included on the Agency ($199/yr) and Freelancer ($99/yr) plans, with the footnote: "* These features are not currently available, but are planned for the near future." (etchwp.com pricing page)
    • "Self-contained patterns, components, and styling. Everything is portable, shareable, and eventually git-able." (etchwp.com Why Etch? page)

    Checked: 6 November 2026
